## **Eclipse-Modulated Transient Energy Pumping in Near-Polar Low Earth Orbit for Spin-Stabilized Solar Sails**

Matthew Acevski, Shiki Vaahan, Piotr Fil, Herbie Christophers, Debdut Sengupta, Fatima Sajid, Madhav Rajeswar, Ian Poon, Rajveer Daga, Neil Pakrasi, Defne Ertugrul

- Eclipse-modulated SRP can produce bounded transient semi-major-axis gain in near-polar LEO for passive solar sails.
- Gain exists only in a narrow LTDN–pitch region; eclipse causally enables it by suppressing negative-work orbital arcs.
- The effect is self-limiting: apogee pumping, eccentricity growth, and perigee erosion drive eventual breakdown.
